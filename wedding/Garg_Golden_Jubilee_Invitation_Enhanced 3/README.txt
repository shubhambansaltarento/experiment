GARG FAMILY — 50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY INVITATION (REFINED EDITION)

Run locally:
1. Open Terminal in this folder.
2. Run: python3 -m http.server 8000
3. Visit http://localhost:8000

The first tap starts the supplied music. On the invitation scene, tap the envelope to play its opening animation and reveal the Golden Jubilee.
Add to Calendar downloads an ICS event. Explore Venue opens a Google Maps search.
Original uploaded photographs and WAV soundtrack are copied unchanged.
Premium typography uses Google Fonts when online, with serif fallbacks offline.
